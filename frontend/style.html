5. Output styling

Paper size & plan scale

The best-fit paper size and plan scale for this job's extent are suggested automatically the first time you reach this step — pick a different sheet or scale below any time, or click "Use best-fit paper size" to re-suggest.

Marker & text style

Bearing & distance labels

Adds a bearing/distance -TEXT entity along each leg of every boundary polyline (general and PT-only alike), positioned just clear of the line and rotated to run along it — matching the closing traverse preview on the Arrange page. Labels that would overlap another are automatically shifted along their own line, or left out if there's truly no clear spot (the figure is still in the traverse table on the Arrange page either way).

PT boundary polyline

Traces the "PT"-classified points that are also ticked "boundary" on the Arrange page, as their own polyline on a separate layer. Points unticked there are left out automatically — drag to reorder, or remove any point from just this polyline, before generating below.
